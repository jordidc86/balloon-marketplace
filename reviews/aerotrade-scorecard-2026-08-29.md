## Interpretation

The platform can receive either a listing-specific enquiry or unmet buying demand, preserve it if email fails, acknowledge the buyer, compare unmet demand with active supply, and route a buyer who found no suitable used balloon into a contextual new Pasha/Schroeder request. Search/category/country context and the public reference listing can now survive that transition. A daily server-HMAC key connects acquisition, catalogue search, listing view, contact reveal and consented requests without storing the raw browser or user identifier; operator traffic is excluded and old events are honestly labelled unattributed. An opted-in wanted request closes the matching loop automatically without repeating an accepted advert. Seller verification remains seller-requested and atomically audited without retaining original documents. Active listing images, public SEO contracts and the real Premium recovery path are verified. The remaining constraint is validated marketplace liquidity and whether discovery, trust or recovery actually converts to a commercial opportunity.

Observe genuine post-release journeys and connect Search Console coverage/performance evidence to the new search, view, contact, wanted and new-balloon stages. Then recruit supply only against repeated verified demand. Do not infer performance from the 60 legacy views, launch unsolicited outreach or fabricate a quote solely to test the path.
