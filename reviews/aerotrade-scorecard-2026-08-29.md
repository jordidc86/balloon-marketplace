## Interpretation

The platform can receive either a listing-specific enquiry or unmet buying demand, preserve it if email fails, acknowledge the buyer, compare unmet demand with active supply, and route a buyer who found no suitable used balloon into a contextual new Pasha/Schroeder request. A listing enquiry can become a traceable but explicitly non-binding negotiation: the buyer may indicate a price and the owner may continue, counter or decline without AeroTrade reserving equipment, moving money or creating a contract. If an enquiry or new-balloon quote eventually closes, the gross outcome, AeroTrade revenue, evidence snapshot and WON state now commit together; only referenced bank or Stripe evidence counts as settled revenue. On the supply side, an unready owner can enter a private assisted path instead of abandoning the marketplace; the case remains unpublished and can close only through a matching normal listing. Search/category/country context and the public reference listing can survive the buyer transition. A daily server-HMAC key connects acquisition, catalogue search, listing view, contact reveal and consented requests without storing the raw browser or user identifier; operator traffic is excluded and old events are honestly labelled unattributed. An opted-in wanted request closes the matching loop automatically without repeating an accepted advert. Seller verification remains seller-requested and atomically audited without retaining original documents. Active listing images, public SEO contracts and the real Premium recovery path are verified. The remaining constraint is validated marketplace liquidity and whether discovery, trust or recovery actually converts to a commercial opportunity.
